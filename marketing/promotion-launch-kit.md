## Positioning
**Audience:** independent freelancers who need to estimate project prices and prepare client-facing quotes or invoices.
**Free value:** in-browser project pricing calculator, deposit calculator, and practical articles. No login required for the project calculator.
**Optional paid offer:** downloadable HTML-based quote/invoice toolkit, local draft storage, printable documents, one-time introductory purchase. Not accounting software.
**Primary call to action:** try the free calculator first.
