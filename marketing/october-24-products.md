# Three new Wizekin tools — 24 October 2026

These are original educational resources on the Wizekin site. External launch posts have not been published by this change. Do not count drafts as traffic or sales.

## FrameMint

Audience: makers preparing their own product listing photos. Main query: free product photo resizer. Related question: when should I use fit instead of fill?

Free tool: https://wizekin.web.app/framemint/
Useful guide: https://wizekin.web.app/framemint/guides/fit-vs-fill-product-photos.html

Prepared maker introduction: I built FrameMint as a small browser-local photo tool. It adds a consistent canvas around product photos, offers fit and fill, and exports ordinary image files. It does not remove backgrounds. The free single-image workflow needs no account; there is an optional offline edition for batches and saved settings.

## StudyMap

Audience: learners arranging their own study schedule. Main query: free study timetable generator. Related question: how can I balance several subjects in limited study time?

Free tool: https://wizekin.web.app/studymap/
Useful guide: https://wizekin.web.app/studymap/guides/build-a-balanced-study-timetable.html

Prepared maker introduction: I built StudyMap to turn available days and subject priorities into a clear timetable. The free planner prints and exports CSV or a calendar file. It does not read your calendar or guarantee grades. An optional offline edition adds saved settings and local completion marks.

## LabelLeaf

Audience: makers printing simple product descriptions on plain paper. Main query: free printable product label maker. Related question: how do I print labels at actual size?

Free tool: https://wizekin.web.app/labelleaf/
Useful guide: https://wizekin.web.app/labelleaf/guides/print-product-labels-at-actual-size.html

Prepared maker introduction: I built LabelLeaf for a small finishing touch on handmade work: product names, a detail, a price and a maker line. The free tool makes A4 and Letter cut sheets. It is not an adhesive-brand template, barcode generator or regulated labeling system. The offline edition adds different labels in one run and saved settings.
