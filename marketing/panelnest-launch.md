## Product description

PanelNest is a free rectangular sheet cutting planner for small workshops. Enter stock, finished part sizes, quantities, blade kerf and edge trim. Control which parts can rotate, compare twelve deterministic layouts, and export diagrams, part lists and printable plans. Measurements stay in your browser.

Try it: https://wizekin.web.app/panelnest/

Read the method: https://wizekin.web.app/panelnest/guides/sheet-cutting-with-kerf-and-grain.html

## Educational launch copy

Four 50 × 50 parts appear to fit a 100 × 100 sheet—until you include the blade. With a 3-unit kerf, two neighboring parts need 103 units of space. PanelNest makes that hidden allowance visible, along with edge trim, grain locks and offcut rectangles.

The free planner tries twelve layouts and explains what is included in its material totals. It does not guarantee a mathematical optimum or generate CNC toolpaths. SVG, CSV, JSON job backups and all-sheet printing are included without an account.

An optional standalone Offline Workshop adds saved jobs, larger jobs, stock comparison, labels and DXF planning outlines. The core public tool remains free.
