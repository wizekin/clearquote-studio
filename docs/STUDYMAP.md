# StudyMap

Audience: Students and independent learners arranging their own study time.

Create a balanced study timetable from your subjects, study days and session lengths. Export a printable schedule, CSV or calendar file without signup.

Free URL: https://wizekin.web.app/studymap/
Guide: https://wizekin.web.app/studymap/guides/build-a-balanced-study-timetable.html

Optional extension: Keep up to 50 named timetable settings, tick off completed sessions on your device, and export or restore JSON backups. Generate and export schedules without an internet connection.

No accounts, external APIs, ads or server-side customer data. Product files for sale are maintained outside this public repository. Product claims and limitations are stated in the page FAQs. No sales, traffic or search rankings are assumed.
