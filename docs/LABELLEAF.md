# LabelLeaf

Audience: Makers preparing simple descriptive labels.

Make clean printable product labels with names, details, price text and a maker line. Preview A4 or Letter cut sheets and print or save as PDF.

Free URL: https://wizekin.web.app/labelleaf/
Guide: https://wizekin.web.app/labelleaf/guides/print-product-labels-at-actual-size.html

Optional extension: Create up to 42 different labels on one run, save reusable sheet settings, and back up or restore your presets. Works from one standalone HTML file in your browser.

No accounts, external APIs, ads or server-side customer data. Product files for sale are maintained outside this public repository. Product claims and limitations are stated in the page FAQs. No sales, traffic or search rankings are assumed.
