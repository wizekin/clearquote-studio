# FrameMint

Audience: Independent makers preparing original product photos.

Resize product photos, fit or crop them to a clean background, adjust brightness and export JPEG, PNG or WebP. Local browser processing, no signup.

Free URL: https://wizekin.web.app/framemint/
Guide: https://wizekin.web.app/framemint/guides/fit-vs-fill-product-photos.html

Optional extension: Batch-export up to 12 images as one ZIP, save reusable photo settings, and export or restore JSON backups. One standalone HTML file; no installation or subscription.

No accounts, external APIs, ads or server-side customer data. Product files for sale are maintained outside this public repository. Product claims and limitations are stated in the page FAQs. No sales, traffic or search rankings are assumed.
