A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear answers.

Will this align with my adhesive label sheets?

No. It uses its own cut-sheet grid with 10 mm page margins and 3 mm gaps. It does not implement a specific manufacturer’s template. Use plain paper and trim the labels.

Can I save a PDF?

Use Print and choose your browser’s Save as PDF option where available. Select the same paper size, 100% scale, and disable headers and footers.

Does it make barcodes or compliance labels?

No. These are simple descriptive product labels. They do not create barcodes or satisfy food, cosmetics, safety or other regulated labeling requirements.

Can I use the printed labels for my shop?

Yes. You are responsible for accurate claims and for using text you have the right to use. The tool does not certify products or verify their ingredients.
