How the deposit is calculated

The formula is deposit = total price × deposit percentage ÷ 100. The remaining balance is the agreed total minus the deposit. For example, a $900 project with a 30% deposit means $270 paid upfront and $630 remaining.

Before requesting a deposit, agree on when it is due, when the rest is due, what counts as project acceptance, and any applicable refund terms. The deposit is part of the project total—not an additional fee unless explicitly agreed.
