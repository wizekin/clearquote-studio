A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear answers.

Does this remove backgrounds?

No. It adds a colored canvas around your image and can crop or fit it. Existing backgrounds remain part of your photo.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. This tool decodes and draws images in your browser. Ordinary website access logs may still be processed by the host. Purchases use Gumroad separately.

What about image quality and metadata?

Avoid enlarging small images. Browser canvas exports re-encode your image and generally discard source metadata; color and orientation may vary by browser. Check the final output.

Can I use it for commercial product photos?

Yes, use your own photos or photos you have permission to edit. Do not use it to misrepresent a product. The illustrated sample is only a demonstration.
