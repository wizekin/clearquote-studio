A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear answers.

How are subjects distributed?

For each session, the planner chooses the subject furthest below its weighted share so far. Rounding means short schedules may not exactly match the priority ratios.

Will it avoid calendar clashes?

No. It creates a new plan from your input and does not read your calendar. Check for clashes before importing. It is not a reminder service.

What timezone does the calendar use?

Calendar files use floating local times. Your calendar interprets them in its own timezone. Import once to avoid duplicates, and review dates and times after importing.

Does this guarantee better grades?

No. It arranges study time. It does not assess learning, choose a curriculum, or guarantee academic outcomes.
