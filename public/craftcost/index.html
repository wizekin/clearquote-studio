Build the price from the batch.

Start with materials, add an optional allowance, then include the time and overhead for the whole batch. Packaging is entered per sellable item.

Price = (unit cost + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − target margin − percentage fee). Percentages are fractions of the selling price. A 30% margin is different from a 30% markup.

A worked example

30 materials + 10% material allowance = 33. Add 24 labor, 6 overhead and 10 packaging: the batch costs 73, or 7.30 per item for 10 sellable items. With a 30% target margin, 5% fee and 0.30 fixed fee, the price is 11.69 per item before rounding.

Before you use the estimate

Does material allowance mean defective finished products?

No. It adds a percentage to material cost. Enter only the sellable output in the batch quantity. Account for failed pieces there; do not count the same loss twice.

Are taxes, shipping, and marketplace fees automatic?

No. Enter the fees that apply to your sale. Allocate shipping and overhead yourself. Fixed fees assume one item per transaction; split an order-level fee when an order contains several items. Figures are planning estimates, not accounting or tax advice.

Can I use any currency?

Yes. Use one currency for every monetary input. No exchange rate conversion is performed. Compare the result with what customers will pay; a calculated price does not guarantee sales.
