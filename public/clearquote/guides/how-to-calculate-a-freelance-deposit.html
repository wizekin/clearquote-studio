How to Calculate a Freelance Project Deposit

Calculate upfront deposits, balances and installment amounts with transparent examples.

A deposit helps clarify what the client pays before work begins. The right percentage depends on your agreement, the type of project, customer relationship and local rules. The calculation itself is straightforward.

Deposit formula

Deposit = total agreed project price × (deposit percentage ÷ 100).

Example: 30% deposit on $900

$900 × 0.30 = $270 deposit. The balance due later is $900 − $270 = $630. The combined payments should total $900, not $1,170.

Write the terms clearly

Specify whether the deposit is refundable under your agreement and applicable law, when remaining amounts are due, how milestones are approved and whether expenses or taxes are included in the total.

Deposits are not the same as profit

Even if you collect $270 upfront, the project may involve labor, expenses, taxes and refunds or other obligations. Do not treat the entire deposit as net earnings.
