01 / Settings

Practice without extra setup.

Choose an operation, operand range, and number of problems. Generate a sheet, then print the worksheet and its separate answer key. Choose “Worksheet only” if you do not want to print answers.

The same seed and settings reproduce the same sheet. Change the seed for another version. Random selection can repeat a problem; uniqueness is not guaranteed.

Know what the range means.

Addition, subtraction and multiplication start with operands in your range. Subtraction is rearranged to avoid negative answers. For exact division, the range applies to the divisor and quotient; the dividend can therefore be larger than the maximum.

Questions teachers and parents ask

Can I print an answer key separately?

Yes. The key is on a separate page. Use the worksheet-only print button or select the answer page in your browser print dialog. Print output varies with browser, paper size, margins and scale; check the preview.

Is this tied to a particular curriculum?

No. It is arithmetic practice, not an assessment or curriculum certification. Choose difficulty based on the learner’s readiness. No student details are collected or saved by the free tool.

Need a reusable workspace?

The offline edition adds saved work and reusable exports. This free tool remains available without a purchase.
